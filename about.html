About me

Most tech products don't struggle because they lack innovation. They struggle because people don't understand them, and increasingly, because AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews don't understand them either.

I'm an SEO, AEO and GEO strategist. For [8+] years I've written and managed content for fintech, Web3 and AI companies, with one goal: making sure the right people find the product, whether they search on Google or ask a chatbot.

I've also worked on the other side of AI. That work showed me how language models process text, which is what AEO and GEO depend on.
